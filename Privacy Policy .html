Privacy Policy for MathRush Pro

Last Updated: May 19, 2026

1. Introduction

MathRush Pro ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

MathRush Pro is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All data, including your practice history, scores, streaks, and settings, is stored locally on your device only.

3. Local Data Storage

The app stores the following information locally on your device using device storage:

  • Practice scores and accuracy statistics
  • Streak counts and achievement progress
  • User preferences and difficulty settings
  • App state (premium status, trial information)

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third parties.

4. No Internet Connection Required

MathRush Pro functions entirely offline. No internet connection is required for core gameplay. Network access is only used if you choose to make an in-app purchase or restore purchases, which is handled securely by Apple's StoreKit.

5. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. The app complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Since no data is collected, there is no personal information to protect.

6. In-App Purchases

The app offers a premium subscription and free trial. All payment processing is handled securely by Apple through their StoreKit system. We do not have access to your payment information.

7. Third-Party Services

MathRush Pro does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking services.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the app and on this page with an updated date.
